VEL Stainless — Brand & Poster Design

We designed the complete visual identity for VEL Stainless — a stainless steel scrubber brand — including their logo, and a series of promotional posters used across retail partners like Heber Traders and other distributors. Each poster was tailored with dealer-specific contact details while keeping the core brand look consistent.

What We Delivered
  • Logo design for the VEL Stainless brand (peacock-feather mark + wordmark)
  • A reusable poster template system — swap product shots, taglines, and dealer contact info per retailer
  • Retail-ready in-store display posters used by multiple distributors across Tamil Nadu
  • Consistent brand colors, typography, and tagline ("Real Clean. Real Value.") across every asset

What We Delivered

  • Photorealistic product renders for e-commerce and lifestyle marketing
  • Retail shelf-display posters and promotional creatives ("Now with Turbo Boosters", 50% More Effective callouts)

  • Dark, cinematic background treatment with camera & tripod hero imagery to match the studio's visual style
  • Clear contact call-to-action with WhatsApp icon for quick booking inquiries
