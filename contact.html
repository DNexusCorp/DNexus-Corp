FAQ

Questions Before
You Reach Out

Still unsure? Just WhatsApp us — it's faster than email for quick questions.

Where exactly is DNexus Corp located?

Our office is at Malaikovil, Thiruverumbur, Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu, India. You can see the exact pin on the map above. We work with clients in person occasionally but mostly over calls, since most of our client base is spread across Tamil Nadu and beyond anyway.

How fast will I actually hear back?

Within 24 hours, almost always sooner on business days. WhatsApp messages tend to get the quickest replies since that's where our team checks most often during work hours. Weekend submissions get picked up first thing Monday.

Is the consultation really free, or is there a catch?

It's free, no catch. We use the first call to understand what you actually need — sometimes that means recommending a smaller scope than what you asked for, because that's what makes sense for your budget and timeline. We'd rather earn a long-term client than oversell a first project.

Do I need to visit your office, or can everything be done remotely?

Everything can be done remotely — design reviews, development updates, campaign reporting, all of it. Most of our clients across Tamil Nadu and internationally have never visited the Trichy office and that's completely fine. If you're nearby and want to meet in person, we're happy to arrange that too.

What information should I include when I reach out?

The more specific, the better. Tell us what your business does, what problem you're trying to solve (new website, more leads, an app idea, AI search visibility), and a rough sense of timeline or budget if you have one. Vague briefs take longer to scope properly.

Can DNexus Corp handle urgent or rush projects?

Sometimes, depending on what's already on our plate. Mention the urgency clearly in your message or call directly rather than emailing — we'll tell you honestly within the first conversation whether your timeline is realistic for the scope you need.
