Can DNexus Corp handle all my digital needs without other agencies?

Yes, that's the point. We cover website design and development, mobile apps, UI/UX, graphic design, billing software, SEO, GEO, AEO, social media, Google Ads, performance marketing, and branding — all internally. You work with one team, one point of contact, one invoice.

What is the difference between SEO, GEO, and AEO?

SEO gets your website ranking on Google's traditional search results. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) gets your brand mentioned in AI-generated answers on ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) wins Google Featured Snippets and voice search answers. All three work differently and address different search formats — running them together gives you the most complete visibility.

How long does a website project take?

A static website or landing page: 1–2 weeks. A dynamic website with CMS, booking, or user accounts: 4–6 weeks. A full e-commerce platform: 6–10 weeks depending on product volume and integrations. Complex web applications are scoped individually after a discovery call.

Do you work with businesses outside Trichy and Tamil Nadu?

Yes. We have a fully remote-compatible workflow — scheduled calls, shared project management, and regular async updates. We currently serve clients in Tamil Nadu, across India, and internationally in the US, UK, UAE, Canada, and Australia. Time zones haven't been an issue.

What does a Flutter app project cost?

Flutter app pricing depends on complexity — number of screens, backend integrations, third-party APIs, and whether we're also building the backend server. Contact us for a free scoping session where we'll break down a realistic estimate based on your specific requirements.

Will I own the source code and accounts after the project?

Yes, completely. Once your project is paid and delivered, you own 100% of the source code, design files, and creative assets. For marketing services, all ad accounts, analytics properties, and social profiles are in your name from the start — not ours.
