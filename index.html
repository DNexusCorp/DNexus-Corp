What services does DNexus Corp offer?

We offer website design & development, mobile app development (Flutter, native), UI/UX design, graphic design, billing software, SEO, GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), social media marketing & management, Google Ads, performance marketing, and business/personal branding — all in-house, worldwide.

What is GEO — Generative Engine Optimization?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of optimizing your brand so it appears in AI-generated answers on ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot. As AI search grows, GEO is critical for being discovered by users who ask AI tools instead of typing into Google. DNexus Corp is one of the pioneers of GEO services in India.

What is AEO — Answer Engine Optimization?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) focuses on structuring your website content to win Google Featured Snippets, voice search results, and AI assistant responses from Siri, Alexa, and Google Assistant. DNexus Corp provides full AEO implementation — schema markup, speakable tags, structured Q&A content — making your brand the definitive answer.

How long does it take to build a website?

A standard static or landing-page website takes 1–2 weeks. Dynamic websites with CMS or e-commerce functionality take 4–6 weeks. Complex custom web applications are scoped individually based on feature requirements.

Does DNexus Corp serve international clients?

Absolutely. DNexus Corp serves clients worldwide — from SMBs in India to enterprises in the US, UK, UAE, Canada, and beyond. Our async-first workflow handles any time zone efficiently with regular progress updates.

Do I own 100% of the source code after delivery?

Yes — completely. Once your project is fully paid and delivered, you own all intellectual property including source code, design files, and creative assets. No vendor lock-in, ever.
